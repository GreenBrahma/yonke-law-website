Compliance guardrails

Every ad and post passes attorney review before it serves. Missouri (Rules 4-7.1 to 4-7.5) and Kansas (KRPC 7.1 to 7.5) govern lawyer advertising; the approving attorney is the final authority, and the current rule text should be confirmed before launch.

  • No false or misleading claims, and no guarantee of outcome
  • Past results and settlement amounts carry the required disclaimer, or are left out
  • No "specialist" or "expert" language unless the attorney holds a recognized certification
  • Testimonials and endorsements are real, consented, and not typical-result claims
  • Firm name and a responsible attorney or office location appear where required
  • Any state-specific disclaimer language is present
  • No direct or targeted solicitation of specific accident victims, including by WhatsApp or direct message
  • Approved final version and approver saved to the Google Drive archive

Naming conventions

Every campaign, ad set and ad uses the same pattern, in capitals, joined by underscores. Launch timing lives on the Planning Wall, not in the name.

Campaign:  YL_[CHANNEL]_[CASETYPE]_[GEO]_[OBJECTIVE]
Ad set:    [Campaign]_[AUDIENCE]
Ad:        [Ad set]_[CREATIVE]_V[#]

Example:   YL_META_AUTO_KCMETRO_LEAD_RETARGET30_VIDEOMIKE_V2
FieldCodes
ChannelGADS (Google Search), GPMAX, GLSA (Local Services), YT, META (Facebook and Instagram), WA (WhatsApp), TT, LI, RDT, YELP, GBP, WEB, PORTAL, SUB (Substack), BL (Best Lawyers), SL (Super Lawyers)
Case typeAUTO, TRUCK, MOTO, PREM (premises), WD (wrongful death), WC (workers' comp), BRAND, GEN
GeoKCMETRO, MO, KS, MOKS
ObjectiveLEAD, CALL, AWARE (awareness), RETARG (retargeting), REVIEW
AudienceShort descriptor: RETARGET30, LOOKALIKE, AGE25-54, KEYWORD
CreativeShort descriptor plus version: VIDEOMIKE_V1, STATIC-CRASH_V3

Files in Google Drive follow the same codes: YL_META_AUTO_VIDEOMIKE_V2_APPROVED.mp4.

UTM standards

Every link to Yonke.Law from outside the site carries UTM tags, so GA4 can tie each visit to its campaign. All values lowercase, hyphens instead of spaces.

ParameterRuleValues
utm_sourceThe platformgoogle, facebook, instagram, whatsapp, youtube, tiktok, linkedin, reddit, yelp, gbp, substack, bestlawyers, superlawyers, chatgpt, perplexity
utm_mediumThe kind of trafficcpc, paid-social, paid-video, organic-social, listing, directory, email, referral
utm_campaignThe campaign name, lowercaseyl_meta_auto_kcmetro_lead
utm_contentThe ad or creativevideomike_v2
utm_termSearch keyword (Google Ads fills it automatically){keyword}
 
https://yonke.law/car-accident?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid-social&utm_campaign=yl_meta_auto_kcmetro_lead&utm_content=videomike_v2
  • Google Business Profile website link: ?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=listing&utm_campaign=yl_gbp_brand
  • Directory profiles (Best Lawyers, Super Lawyers, Yelp): utm_medium=directory
  • Never put UTM tags on links within Yonke.Law; it resets the visit's source
  • Google Ads uses auto-tagging; add UTMs through the account's tracking template only
